# Research source marks

These marks identify the named research services; they do not indicate endorsement or a connection status. They are served locally so displaying them sends no request to a provider.

`provenance.json` records each original provider-owned asset URL and SHA-256 digest. Downloaded 17 October 2026. Preserve original colors and aspect ratios. Google Patents uses Google's site icon; PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov use their official site identity icons. Internal actions (deep research, uploaded files, dataset discovery) retain Lucide symbols because they are workflows, not individual brands.

Brand assets remain subject to their owners' trademark/copyright terms and are not covered by the application's MIT license:

- [Zotero artwork and trademark guidance](https://www.zotero.org/support/brand)
- [Python logo usage](https://www.python.org/community/logos/)
- [arXiv name and logo use](https://info.arxiv.org/brand/brand-guidelines.html)
- [Google brand guidance](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/)
- [Mendeley Developer Portal](https://dev.mendeley.com/)
- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/), [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/), [Grants.gov](https://www.grants.gov/)

If an asset cannot load, the component retains a recognizable action symbol and the adjacent text label.
